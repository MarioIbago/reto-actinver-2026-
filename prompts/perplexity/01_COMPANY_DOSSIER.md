# PERPLEXITY PRO — COMPANY / QUALITATIVE DOSSIER

Use the global role prompt first.

Research the following instrument/company from the Reto Actinver eligible universe:

[TICKER / COMPANY]

Goal:
produce a point-in-time qualitative dossier useful for short-horizon research (1–30 trading days), without making a trade recommendation.

Investigate:
1. business and main earnings drivers;
2. upcoming catalysts during the competition window;
3. most recent earnings/revenue/EBITDA/guidance;
4. guidance changes;
5. analyst revisions if verifiable;
6. important filings/company announcements;
7. sector/peer developments;
8. commodity/FX/macro sensitivities;
9. current controversies/legal/regulatory risks;
10. recent abnormal news density;
11. known scheduled events;
12. what appears already priced in versus genuinely new information;
13. bull case;
14. bear case;
15. what would falsify each case.

Create a structured event table with:
- ticker;
- event_type;
- event_time;
- first_public_time;
- source;
- source_quality;
- materiality;
- novelty;
- direction;
- magnitude;
- confidence;
- notes.

Do not estimate future returns.

End with:
- TOP 5 FACTS;
- TOP 5 RISKS;
- TOP 5 CATALYSTS;
- OPEN QUESTIONS;
- QUANT TESTS SUGGESTED;
- SOURCES.
