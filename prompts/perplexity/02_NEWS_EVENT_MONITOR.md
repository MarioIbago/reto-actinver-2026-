# PERPLEXITY PRO — NEWS / EVENT SCAN

Use the global role prompt first.

This is a MANUAL scan unless your account visibly supports scheduled tasks.

Scope:
ONLY the instruments in the supplied Reto Actinver eligible universe.

Objective:
detect genuinely material NEW information that could matter over 1–10 trading days.

Track:
- earnings;
- guidance;
- preannouncements;
- M&A;
- buybacks;
- management changes;
- debt/refinancing;
- regulatory decisions;
- major contracts;
- litigation;
- production/volume changes;
- analyst upgrades/downgrades;
- commodity shocks;
- FX shocks;
- macro surprises;
- sector spillovers;
- relevant US-company read-throughs;
- official Actinver rule/universe changes.

For every detected event:
1. confirm it from the strongest available source;
2. identify first-public timestamp;
3. avoid duplicates/syndicated copies;
4. distinguish new information from commentary;
5. identify affected tickers;
6. assign materiality LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH;
7. assign novelty LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH;
8. explain transmission mechanism in 2–4 sentences;
9. record contradictory information.

Return ONLY events that pass a materiality threshold.

Output schema:
timestamp
ticker
event_type
headline
source
source_url
source_quality
novelty
materiality
direction
confidence
why_it_matters
what_to_verify_next

Do not issue trade instructions.
